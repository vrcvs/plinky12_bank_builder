Plinky 12 Bank Builder

Multisample banks for Plinky 12, made in your browser. By VRCVS

Unofficial tool, use at your own risk. Not supported by plinkysynth. Questions or bugs: Instagram DM @vrcvs or vrcvsvrcvs@gmail.com. The author is not responsible for damage to your hardware, memory card or data. Back up your card first.

1 · Load your samples

Drop your sample files here or click to choose them (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, OGG). Up to 8 files, one per Plinky slot.

The note is read from the file name: piano_C3.wav, Bb2 long.wav, sax-060.wav. Any note works: the bank plays each file on its own note.

3 · Create the bank

Each bank gets 8 presets (00 to 07), one per variation, all using your samples. Total audio is limited to about 65 s, shared between the samples, so long samples are shortened automatically.

No folder chosen

The bank is written as artists/<ARTIST>/<BANK>/ inside the folder you choose. Copy that artists folder into PLINKY/BANKS/ on the card (keep a backup first). The app only writes where you choose.

Your samples: you load them and you decide what to use. You are responsible for having the rights to every sample you load. Nothing is uploaded: all processing happens locally in your browser.
